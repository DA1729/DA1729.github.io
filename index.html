Helloo... this is my grand and ultimate website. It contains a LOT of stuff, and I have not done a good job naming the pages to explain themselves, although if you wear your specs, you should see little descriptions. Anyway, here you will find, in a nutshell, 5% of the things I think about. Even 5% seems like a big number given the amount of stuff we think about and are influenced by; the density is “unimaginable” (idk if I intend a pun here).

I really like to write and present my raw pieces of work and half-baked thoughts and workouts. The content on this website really lives on the entire spectrum of formal and casual. To test this, you will see a link [Surprise me] which opens a random article or object on this website. Do it a couple of times, and you should see both formal and casual articles, with casual outnumbering the former (I didn’t say the distribution was uniform lol).

Moving on, I would really appreciate anyone exploring this website however they want. And PLEASE, always read it with a fair amount of ambiguity and open-thought. I try to present my abstract thoughts here a lot, and I really don’t think they can ever be presented through words alone. You need the words, in this case, to establish a direct communication channel between my mind and the mind of whoever’s reading any of my stuff.

A little about me -- I really like mathematics, and the rest you can read on my portfolio page.
