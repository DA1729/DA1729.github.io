# Chopin Airport

Ok, I am currently at the Chopin airport, Warsaw, waiting for my flight to India. This airport, to be very honest, does not feel like an airport at all, mostly because of all the stuff going on in my head this past week. I just finished my internship with COSIC, and it was AMAZING. I did some cool stuff, and now the only thing left is to write a paper and just submit. I have a pretty good feeling, given the work and the results, that it should land somewhere nice, and I will post the pre-print in a few weeks.

Another thing I am really happy about is that I am coming back to Leuven in the winters. This alone made this last week much easier for me, because of the way I spend time with a place: just walking everywhere, just letting the place absorb me, not me absorbing the place. That makes leaving them really hard, especially when these past 12 weeks were nothing but consistent, harmonious, constructive interference between my wavelength and the town's wavelength. Yeah, so this makes leaving such places really hard for me, even though it might not look like I am even caring. BUT, knowing that I will be back during the winters (like literally only 12 weeks later ahahha) took off a lot of the burden.

I have a lot of things I want and can write right now, and I have the time on me, and on the plane as well, since I have the aisle seat this time, so no pressure from my inner-child to constantly keep looking out the window. But it's always fun to look over the Himalayas and all the valleys you get after crossing the Middle-East, anyway, in winters hopefully. Since I have a lot to write, I might just write a few paragraphs on what order I am going to write things in. But wait a minute, I think it will be an interesting post/entry if I just keep writing as I think, should I do that? That will certainly be easier for me, but I'm really curious about the brain exercises you do when you actually organize your thoughts for writing purposes. Honestly, I am not going to think much over it, and I am just going to write as I think, because I know that I won't have to spend a lot of energy thinking, because I have been thinking with a very high density this past week, and I sort of have that content up there. Expect some random/seemingly unrelated stuff further ahead.

---

First, I just have to document how I actually managed to literally gaslight myself this week. So, my regular café in Leuven is Madmum's, and specifically the one on Tiensestraat (I have things to write about Tiensestraat as well, in later paragraphs). My order is one cold-brew, mostly takeaway, while I walked to office everyday, but on this particular weekend, I sat down outside by the street and enjoyed the most refreshing cold-brew ever. So, during my last 2 weeks of the internship, one thing I was starting to get really gloomy about was that there would be this last day -- August 27 (now this is what I thought my last day there would be, it'll be important later lol), and maybe telling the baristas there, not my last day, but that I will now see them in November. It's a less gloomy situation in itself, compared to the former and what could've been, but still, given a preference, I would have not preferred being in the situation at all. Anyway, I just kept on going there, with those short exchanges of greetings every morning, and that thing used to be one of the highlights of the day. Saying hello to everyone, the barista already knowing my order, then there was this one barista who sort of looked like the cool divorced mom of the entire café, she's the sweetest, then there were these 3 guys, amazing. I startled one of them this other day, when I went for a long black instead of a cold-brew. Not my fault, it was a chilly and rainy day, and I was already a little wet, and I really did not want to get sick during my time in Belgium (I did not, thankfully). Then came the 27th of August. I woke up, my mind surrounded with "the 2nd last day for everything" (lab, café, FNAC, properly spending time in Leuven, etc.). My last meeting with the supervisors was also on that day, so I just got up, did my morning chores, then headed towards Madmum's. Constantly my mind saying -- second last day, second last day, .... I genuinely thought it was my last day. But right when I entered the café, my mind all of a sudden just revealed we won't be coming here tomorrow, so if you want to say bye -- if you can, cuz you've never really done it before -- now's the only time. So I went to the counter, hoping to strike up a conversation with the one taking my order, but he just knew and said that your order will be ready in a minute, which as usual made me happy, but that was not the thing I was looking forward to the most at that moment. Then the 'cool divorced-mom' prepared my order, and I just went ahead, took it, and I think she was almost on the track to say -- enjoy your drink, bye! I went ahead and said -- I will be seeing y'all in November now. She could not hear me properly the first time, because it was clear that she was not really ready for some out-of-the-usual conversation, she nodded curiously, then went, and I said again -- "I will be seeing y'all in November now." Then there was this really micro-moment, felt like a feeling that lasted, the feeling was fear of coming off as awkward and all, but spending time in a whole 'nother country and dealing with all the new stuff, meeting very different and a lot of people, really helped me with my social skills, and I actually enjoy having them, it genuinely makes me feel better.

Either way, the situation did not turn out awkward AT ALL. She was all into knowing about why I won't be here in Leuven, then we had a nice chat for a minute or two. Then as I was heading out, putting the lid on my takeaway cup and getting a straw, she told the one who took my order, then had a chat with him as well, then I left the café with one of the best feelings ever. It felt REALLY nice. Now if you're curious about how I got my cold-brew on the 27th? Well, I went to the Madmum's on Brusselstraat, which is actually nearer to where I lived, but I still went the longer way to work everyday, because for the first 2 months I lived on Tiensestraat and the Madmum's there is the one I am really attached to. Brusselstraat is good, but idk, it feels shallower than the one on Tien, which is like literally the best thing ever. And during the dark mornings, they had their golden lights on inside, that's some next-level atmosphere, and I cannot wait to see that everyday during the winters.

My flight for the winters is on 26th November (literally the next day after my end-sem exams lol), and I will land in Brussels at around 6pm, then check in for the first two days in the Leuven City Hostel, exactly where I spent the first two days this time (yes I did this on purpose :) ), then right at 8am the next morning, i.e., 27th November, I WILL find myself at Madmum's, and I cannot wait to do this. I have already given them the heads up that I WON'T be drinking cold-brew in winters.

Now let's see what I want to write. Yeah, about what you expect something will feel like and how it actually turns out. This is such a "mystical" thing to think about. Remember all those times when you were, well, not necessarily "looking-forward," but knew something is coming, like some travel, vacation, exams, work, anything, and you have no choice but to look forward to it, enthusiastically or not. You start forming these feeling-images, in other words, imagining yourself already there, and seeing how the mental simulation feels, then just keep expecting that. Sometimes it's excitement, sometimes anxious, sometimes gloomy, sometimes 'as plain as those diet biscuits (ughh).' And that thing, depending upon the "intensity" of it, could feel almost fictional to you, like that will never happen, and then all of a sudden it just happens, and as the days get closer the farther it feels, ifykwim.

And during these two orthogonally contrasting amplitudes just getting bigger and bigger, you do not realize it, but you just find yourself there, and your actual feeling is way different from what you were expecting. Idk what exact feeling I was expecting for my travel back from Belgium, but I am certainly NOT feeling that right now, it's something completely different. It's just weird. Like very weird. Also given the fact that I will have to grind HARD having all these courses this semester, and yes I am listing them to actually tell how cooked I am:

- General relativity
- Nuclear astrophysics
- Quantum theory of solids
- Semiconductor microtech
- Abstract Algebra
- Real analysis 2
- Atomic and molecular spectroscopy
- then some geology course

Yeah, it's bad, but it's also like, I can kind of utilize the 10 days of intense work, but this time knowing what work I have to do. Because the past 12 weeks were intense work, and every day showing up with the load of finding out what the heck I am supposed to even work upon (this deserves a separate post, and my luck works for me in very unconventional ways). That's the research process, and I enjoyed it, but I can use a few days of break from it. And I have been in much worse situations (academically speaking) than this one, especially during the last semester. My midsems went by with me entirely succumbed to the peak existential phase of my life, then the ETEs due to, well -- "the ego of mediocre Indian professors." "Why would I talk about the professors of my college?? They are like the most qualified, far from mediocre researchers, each one of them :'| .... and they say mathematicians don't keep legal matters in mind." But yeah, this is a totally different college, which must not be named, explicitly and actively doing pseudo-science, like literally I am not even kidding atp. Apparently, if you do a PhD in India, you are an expert in narrow-minded research, and you justify animal urine as the best thing in the world for everything (yes, including cancer). From a very high level, their argument -- the urine contains some carbon, the best cancer med also contains carbon, hence urine is at least as effective as that (I toned it down a lot, but you get the spirit).

I was talking about how chaotic my acads have been so far, and yeah, I might survive this one, given that I survived last semester, and idc about grades now, I am actually beyond that, I have more important things to care about, and failing courses is hard, so yeah I am good, but still I will have to spend 10 days forcing myself into something I won't really enjoy, then after that, I will have my nice 3-day break, i.e., getting drunk with my friends.

I am ending this post right here, I will start a new one literally right after, because there's about an hour left for boarding, followed by I think a 7 hour flight, yeah, and I am in the zone.
